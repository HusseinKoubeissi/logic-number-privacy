Privacy Policy

Logic Number: Brain Puzzles

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Logic Number: Brain Puzzles, an Android game provided by Koubeissi Games. The Android package name is com.logicnumber.logic_number_brain_puzzles.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, completed levels, unlocked hints and solutions, and preferences such as sound settings locally on your device. This information enables you to continue playing and keep previously unlocked help. The game does not use a developer-operated server to synchronize this progress.

Device or operating-system backup features may retain or restore app data depending on your device settings and the app's backup configuration.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

The game uses Google AdMob to show interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements for hints and solutions. Google Mobile Ads SDK processes information to deliver and measure ads, diagnose performance, and prevent fraud.

Depending on the SDK configuration, applicable requirements, and your privacy choices, this information includes:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information concerning the app and advertising SDK.

This information is collected and shared through Google's advertising services. Advertising-related analytics may occur even though the game does not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent and advertising choices

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Where required, a consent message presents the available choices. You can revisit those choices through the game's privacy options when that option is available.

Your choices and applicable requirements affect advertising data processing. Ads that are not personalized may still involve data processing for delivery, measurement, security, or fraud prevention. You can also manage your advertising ID using the controls available in your Android settings.

4. Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use that information to respond to your inquiry, provide support, or address a privacy request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store these communications.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data is retained until it is removed, for example by clearing the app's storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app. Clearing storage removes your saved progress and unlocked help. Copies in device backups may be retained separately and can be managed through your backup provider.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request its deletion by contacting us.

Google determines retention of information processed by its services under its policies and applicable settings. Clearing the game's local data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google. Consult Google's privacy resources for its controls and request procedures.

6. Security and processing locations

Local data is subject to your device's security protections. Google documents that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, as explained in their privacy policies.

7. Privacy requests and children's privacy

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent. Contact us regarding information we hold, such as support correspondence. For information processed by Google, its privacy resources provide additional options. Where applicable, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child's information in connection with this game, contact us so we can review the concern and address information within our control.

8. This policy page and external links

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page contains no added advertising or analytics scripts. External websites and advertisements are governed by their respective providers' privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the game or data practices. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.
